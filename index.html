Example workflow

A call should not end with “someone will get back to you” when the system can resolve the next step now.

This is the operational story a call-centre or reception manager needs to see: receive, understand, act, update and escalate.

01
Receive

Phone, website, WhatsApp, email or form starts the workflow.

02
Understand

AI identifies the request, asks needed questions and uses approved business knowledge.

03
Act

Book, qualify, update, create a case, route, notify or trigger the approved next step.

04
Update systems

Push the right information into CRM, calendar, database, email or internal workflow.

05
Escalate intelligently

Send exceptions to a person with the context already captured.
